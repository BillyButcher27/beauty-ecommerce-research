# Beauty E-commerce Research

## Introduction

This repository contains educational research about the digital experience of beauty and personal care e-commerce websites.

## Research topics

- E-commerce website navigation.
- Beauty product categorisation.
- Online shopping user experience.
- Search engine optimisation.
- Digital content marketing.

## Example Website

Nykaa is an Indian beauty and lifestyle e-commerce platform.

Visit [Nykaa – Beauty Products Online in India](https://www.nykaa.com/) to explore its website and product categories.

## Learning Objective

The objective of this project is to understand how e-commerce websites use website design, content and digital marketing to support online product discovery.
